# PRODUCT

# Inventory Close Gaurd
### The materiality-aware year-end inventory close agent

**Tagline:**

### Every serial. One accounting truth.

**Core positioning:**

Inventory Close Gaurd helps Fast Insights perform a rigorous year-end inventory close across serialized hardware located at warehouses, vendors, carriers, installation sites, customer locations, demo environments, and RMA facilities.

Instead of sending the entire inventory population through expensive AI models, Inventory Close Gaurd uses deterministic accounting controls, reconciliation rules, materiality, and risk scoring to determine where AI reasoning is actually worth the cost.

# Materiality determines AI spend.

The workflow is:

Entire Inventory Population  
↓  
Deterministic Reconciliation  
↓  
Accounting Rules Engine  
↓  
Materiality + Risk Scoring  
↓  
Economy AI Classification  
↓  
Premium AI Investigation  
↓  
Controller Judgment

The core principle is:

# AI investigates. Controllers conclude.
