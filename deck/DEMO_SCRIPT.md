# Transit Guard — 3-Minute Demo Script

Narration + click cues for the video. Works live on **https://transit-guard.vercel.app**
(or `http://localhost:4173`). **Refresh the page before recording** — state is
session-only, so a refresh gives you a clean demo every time.

Prep: full-screen the browser (F11), hide bookmarks (Ctrl+Shift+B), clear old PDFs
from Downloads so the two document downloads are easy to show.

---

**0:00 – 0:15 · Hook** *(app opens on the Plan tab — don't click anything yet)*
> "Inventory counts are perfect on the shelf — the truth gets lost in motion.
> Transit Guard is the chain-of-custody layer: one transaction ID follows every
> shipment, hand-off by hand-off. It complements NetSuite, SAP, Oracle, and
> BlackLine — and what you're seeing is live."

**0:15 – 0:50 · AI vendor search** *(point at the Agent routing policy card, then the package fields; leave defaults — Utrecht, need-by Jan 12, Balanced — and click "Search vendors with AI")*
> "Agents define the route by carrier acceptance of availability, matched on
> weights and measures. Vendor memory is cached — cost per ton, owned lanes —
> only availability is queried per order. I asked for Balanced… but Balanced
> would miss the customer's need-by date, so the agent overrode me and promoted
> the fastest route that makes it."

*(click "Apply route" on the top card)*
> "One tap, and the full custody chain exists."

**0:50 – 1:25 · Carrier add-in — the Uber moment** *(click 🚚 Carrier add-in in the header)*
> "This is the same app from the carrier's side. They see only the shipping
> transaction — never inventory, never values. The agent proposed this load
> like a rideshare…"

*(click "Accept shipment" — then go to the vendor Scan tab and tap Scan)*
> "…accept, scan the shipping label at pickup — docs pack received, custody
> logged, same transaction ID. If a different carrier takes the load, the chain
> auto-updates from the label. And if the change breaks tolerance — more than
> ten percent, two hundred dollars, or two days — a human approves it."

**1:25 – 2:10 · The $1M lane** *(click 🏢 Fast Insights → Track → TX-20499)*
> "One million dollars of perishable product: Palo Alto, New York, across the
> Atlantic, the UK, Poland, Geneva. Minus two degrees, dry ice at every
> hand-off, FOB destination — revenue holds until Geneva accepts."

*(point at the red sender-approval card, click "Approve reroute")*
> "A carrier fell through. The agent re-matched from vendor memory, verified
> delivery timing was unchanged — but the four-hundred-fifty-dollar delta beat
> the tolerance gate, so it stopped and asked the sender. Agents coordinate;
> humans stay in control."

**2:10 – 2:35 · Documents, separately** *(still in a chain: click "Shipping docs (PDF)" on any transport leg, show the download; then click "Customs paperwork (PDF)" on the customs step)*
> "Every stopping point prints its own documents. Shipping docs — packing slip
> and bill of lading. And separately, the customs paperwork: valuation on the
> company's customs price list versus the sales invoice, the terms, and the
> destination checklist — for the UK that means UKCA and CDS, tracked to done."

**2:35 – 3:00 · Close** *(click Flags, pause two seconds, then Audit)*
> "The AI reads the custody chain like a controller: one-point-two-three
> million dollars of revenue exposure flagged, down to a December-versus-January
> tax play. And for the auditors — a count snapshot that ties to the fleet and
> a custody ledger that exports in one tap. Transparency for tax planning,
> revenue recognition, audit-ready documentation, stock planning, and returns.
> Transit Guard — every hand-off, one transaction truth."

### Recording notes

- **Option 4 (fresh recording):** record the browser with any recorder (Loom with
  the mic ON, or Clipchamp's built-in screen recorder) while reading the script.
- **Option 3 (voiceover on the existing video):** open **Clipchamp** (built into
  Windows 11) → Create video → import the downloaded Loom MP4 → drag to the
  timeline → *Record & create → Audio* → record the narration while it plays →
  select the video clip → Audio → volume 0 to mute the original → Export 1080p.
  Share the MP4 directly, or via Google Drive / unlisted YouTube.
